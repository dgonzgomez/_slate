# What's new

### 0.1.4

- Updates: fix the false installation-permission warning on Fire TV and let the system installer check permission after returning from settings.

### 0.1.3

- Wallpapers: add up and down arrows around the trash button to reorder saved wallpapers. The order is saved and focus follows the moved wallpaper.
- Wallpaper controls: give lifted rows more room and remove the bounce to prevent clipping at the sides.

### 0.1.2

- Updates: keep remote focus on the update button as checking and downloading finish.
- Installation: resume the installer after returning from unknown-app permission settings, and give settings time to stay open.

### 0.1.1

- Wallpapers: save a framed Wallhaven image to your library and keep browsing. Delete saved wallpapers from Settings or the picker with the same trash control used by the app rail.
- Wallpaper framing: Save replaces Set, and Menu plus the remote’s direction keys reach the zoom and save controls.
- Home: the app rail no longer reserves empty space when the TV has no HDMI inputs.

### 0.1.0

- Home screen: an app rail with a clock, wallpapers, and settings. Long-press an app or press Menu to move it or remove it from the rail.
- Home button: return to Slate from other apps, with one-time setup over ADB. Support depends on the TV’s Fire OS version.
- Wallpapers: Day and Night are the defaults, with Night based on Graphite. Add your own from Wallhaven using search and a scrolling three-column grid.
- Wallpaper framing: move and zoom the image before pressing Set. Drag to position it, or use Up/Down on the remote. Left/Right or Volume adjusts zoom.
- Inputs: show the HDMI ports the TV exposes. Devices without inputs keep navigation on the app rail.
- Updates: check GitHub from Settings, download a newer version, and confirm the system install. Downloads are checked before installing.
